1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use ReelDramas: Short Drama Story (the “App”). The App allows users to watch short drama videos. The App is provided by BAMECO COMPANY LIMITED (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

2. Information That May Be Processed

The App integrates Google AdMob for advertising and Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics for analytics and crash diagnostics. Depending on the SDK versions, settings, user device, and features used, these services may process categories of information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID where available and permitted by the device and applicable settings.
  • Device and technical information, such as device model, operating system version, app version, language, and network or connectivity information.
  • App usage events and interactions, diagnostic information, crash reports, and performance data.
  • IP address or approximate location inferred from an IP address, where processed by a service provider.
  • Information related to ad delivery, ad measurement, and interactions with advertisements.

The categories above describe information that the integrated services may process; they are not a statement that every category is collected in every session or configuration. We do not ask users to create an account or sign in to use the App, based on the current app design described to us.

The App obtains or streams video content from third-party sources. When video content is requested or played, the relevant service or content provider may receive technical request information, such as an IP address, device/app information, or request details, according to that provider's own practices. The exact information depends on the providers and implementation used by the released App.

The purposes applicable to a particular data category depend on the service, SDK configuration, and feature involved.
